Kenya's own exposure

80% of Kenya's 2035 climate pledge is conditional on international finance that COP30 did not secure.

Kenya's second NDC commits to cutting emissions 35% below business-as-usual by 2035 — 75.25 MtCO₂e. Only 20% is funded domestically. The pledge carries a USD 56 billion price tag. Belém set a target to triple adaptation finance by 2035, with no interim milestone and no burden-sharing formula. The money and the deadline do not line up.

      Disinformation watch

      False and misleading climate & environment narratives circulating in Kenya — tracked so the coalition can coordinate a response. Public-facing debunks are published on raia ↗.

      4 active 1 monitoring 1 debunked Reviewed weekly
      FalseActive

      “Climate change is natural — humans aren’t the cause”

      WhatsApp, X · trending ↑ · Cross-sector

      Counter: The warming tracks our fossil-fuel use exactly; every major science academy agrees. Debunk on raia →

      MisleadingActive

      “Kenya is too poor for climate — development first”

      Political speeches, talk radio · steady · Cross-sector

      Counter: For most Kenyans the environment IS the economy; drought and floods already cost billions a year. Debunk on raia →

      MisleadingMonitoring

      “Carbon credits are free money for communities”

      Project sign-up meetings · localised · Land & carbon

      Counter: Little pay, lost grazing control, decades-long contracts — Kenya’s biggest project was suspended twice. Debunk on raia →

      Missing contextActive

      “The Lamu refinery will make petrol cheap for all”

      Post-announcement shares · rising · Oil & gas

      Counter: Fuel prices follow the global market and owner terms; “60,000 jobs” is a pre-plan promise. Debunk on raia →

      FalseActive

      “Activists are foreign-funded agents blocking progress”
