Privacy Policy

This is a draft privacy notice for the raia.mazingirakenya.org prototype. It will be finalised before public launch.

Who we are

Raia is the public platform of the MazingiraKenya Communications Hub. The data controller is Community Media Trust, P.O. Box 9190–00300, Kirichwa Road, ADK House 1, Kilimani, Nairobi.

What we collect

The reading experience requires no account and collects no personal data. Your chosen language is stored only on your own device. If you submit a story, photo, video or audio, we collect what you send us and your consent to publish it; nothing identifiable is published without that consent.

Your rights

Your data is processed under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and, where applicable, the GDPR. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of anything you have submitted.

Contact

Reach the data controller at the address above regarding any privacy question or request.
