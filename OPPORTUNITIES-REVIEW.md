## 9. COP31 Observer Accreditation (new organisations)
- **Funder / host:** UNFCCC Secretariat
- **Type:** Accreditation
- **Deadline:** Rolling / continuous (secretariat assesses applications on a continuous basis in order of application date; the full review can take a year or more, so applying now is for future COPs, not necessarily COP31 in Nov 2026)
- **Eligibility:** Non-profit / civil-society organisations seeking UNFCCC observer status. Kenyan and African CSOs eligible. Organisations already admitted register delegates via the COP31 Information-for-Participants pages.
